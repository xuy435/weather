ABOUT

This website explores weather as a dynamic system — where text, motion, and atmosphere shift based on real-time conditions.

LOCATION — Click the city name in the top left to search for any city in the world. The background color, animation, and text will update to reflect that city's live weather and local time.

TEMPERATURE — Click the temperature to toggle between °C and °F.

WEATHER — Click the weather label to browse and select from 15 atmospheric states across different cities worldwide.

CUSTOM TEXT — In the location panel, type any phrase and press Add text to inject it into the animation.

CUSTOM MODE — Click Custom Mode in the bottom right to manually control four parameters: weather type (Clear, Wind, Rain, Snow), intensity level (Light, Medium, Heavy), time of day (which shifts the background color and sun position), and temperature.

TIME & COLOR — The background color shifts continuously based on the local time and temperature of the selected city. Daytime colors reflect warmth; night fades into deep blue.
